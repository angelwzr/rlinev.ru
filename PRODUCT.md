# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors looking for Roman Linev's current social profiles and past technology writing, on desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

A compact personal identity and link page. Help visitors recognize Roman Linev, connect the angelwzr alias, and reach social profiles and verified publication archives.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, playful. The cat easter egg provides a small, optional moment of personality.

## Anti-references

Content walls, expanded portfolios, article grids, redundant sections, and dependency-heavy redesigns. Do not replace the existing identity merely to follow a visual trend.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the compact single-page structure and existing identity.
- Make primary links easy to recognize and use.
- Improve details by replacing complexity, not adding it.
- Keep authorship concise and link only known destinations.
- Keep playfulness optional and unobtrusive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: readable contrast, keyboard access and visible focus, usable zoom and small-screen layouts, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.

## Interface Behavior

- The compact layout remains centered when it fits; shorter viewports can scroll vertically.
- Entrance motion enhances visible-by-default content and releases transforms to hover and press states.
- Reduced motion disables entrance, pointer-follow, and ripple animation; cursor glow runs only for fine, hovering pointers and stops when settled.
- Secondary text retains AA contrast in both themes; short authorship text uses balanced wrapping.
- The Konami easter egg uses physical key codes: arrow sequence, then the B and A key positions on a US keyboard, independent of active layout or letter case.
